Account Deletion Request

For Overtime Tracker by Mahmud Apps Official

If you want to delete your account and all associated personal data from Overtime Tracker, please follow the steps below.

How to Request Deletion

Send an email to supporttatisakilabs@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Please send the email from the Google account you used to sign in to the app. This helps us verify your identity.

What Data Will Be Deleted

Once your request is verified, we will permanently delete the following:

  • Your account information (Name and Email address)
  • All app data associated with your account stored in our database (Firebase)

Timeframe

Your account and data will be deleted within 7 business days of receiving your request. We may contact you to verify ownership before processing the deletion.
