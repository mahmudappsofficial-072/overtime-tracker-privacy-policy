Privacy Policy

Welcome to Overtime BD ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application Overtime BD (Package ID: com.overtime.bd).

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the application.

Information We Collect

We only collect information that is strictly necessary to provide you with labor law calculations, overtime tracking, and cloud backup functionality.

A. Information You Provide to Us

  • Personal Information: Name, company name, designation, worker ID, and grade (voluntarily entered by you in your profile).
  • Salary & Compensation Details: Gross salary, basic salary, allowances (house rent, medical, conveyance, food), attendance bonus rules, night bill rate, and tiffin bill rate.
  • Attendance & Overtime Records: Daily overtime hours, shift types (Day, Night, Holiday, Leave, Absent), dates, notes, and piece-rate production records.

B. Information Collected Automatically via Google Services

  • Google Account Data: When you sign in using Google Sign-In, we receive your basic profile information (display name, email address, and unique Google Account Identifier / Firebase UID) through Jetpack Credential Manager.
  • App Integrity & Security Data: We use Firebase App Check and Google Play Services to prevent abuse and verify authentic app requests.

C. Information We DO NOT Collect

  • We do not collect precise GPS or background location.
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, or media gallery.
  • We do not collect any government identification numbers or sensitive banking/credit card credentials.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following operational purposes:

  • Core App Functionality: Calculating accurate monthly overtime wages, absent deductions, allowances, and attendance bonuses in compliance with Bangladesh Labor Law.
  • Generating Pay Slips & Summaries: Creating monthly digital pay-slips and downloadable summary records.
  • Cloud Synchronization & Backup: Safely syncing your records across devices via Google Firebase Firestore when you sign in with your Google account.
  • Historical Rate Protection: Freezing historical overtime snapshots so that future salary increments do not alter past earnings records.
  • Customer Support: Responding to inquiries or WhatsApp support requests initiated by you.

Data Storage, Security, and Retention

  • Local Storage: Your overtime records and profile settings are stored securely on your device using an encrypted local SQLite database (Android Jetpack Room).
  • Cloud Security: When cloud backup is active, data is transferred securely using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS encryption to Google Firebase Firestore servers.
  • Access Control: Firebase Security Rules enforce strict zero-trust authentication; your records can only be read or modified by your authenticated Google account.
  • Data Retention: We retain your data as long as your account remains active. You may clear your data or request account deletion at any time.

Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal or financial data to third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

We only share data with trusted infrastructure providers strictly necessary to operate the application:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): For authentication (Firebase Auth), database hosting (Firestore), and security verification (Firebase App Check). Firebase complies with global security standards (ISO 27001, SOC 1/2/3).

Your Rights & Data Deletion (User Control)

You have full control over your personal data:

  • Offline / Local Mode: You can use the app entirely offline without signing in. In this mode, no data leaves your physical device.
  • Sign Out & Local Reset: Clicking "Sign Out" in the Profile screen immediately removes all locally cached overtime and profile data from your device.
  • Permanent Account & Cloud Data Deletion: If you wish to permanently delete your cloud account and all associated overtime/piece-rate records stored in Firebase Firestore, visit our Account Deletion page at https://mahmudappsofficial-072.github.io/overtime-tracker-bd-account-deletion/ and follow the instructions provided there. After verification, your account and associated cloud data will be permanently deleted within 7 business days, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Children's Privacy

Our application is intended for adult workers, garment employees, and professionals managing their work hours. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements or app features. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy.
