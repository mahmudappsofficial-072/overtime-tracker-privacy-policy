1. Introduction

Tatisaki Labs ("we", "our", or "us") operates Overtime Tracker BD. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use our application.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on your use of the application, the following information may be processed:

  • Account information: Name, email address, and authentication identifiers associated with Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication.
  • Employment information: Salary details, attendance records, overtime hours, overtime earnings, bonuses, and other information you enter into the application.
  • Application data: Records and settings you create or save while using the application.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Authenticate users and manage accounts.
  • Calculate salary, overtime earnings, and attendance.
  • Store and synchronize records associated with user accounts.
  • Support data retrieval and restoration where available.
  • Maintain application security and reliability.

4. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

The application uses Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication, and Cloud Firestore to provide account authentication and data storage. Information handled by these services is also subject to the applicable policies of Google and Firebase.

  • Google Privacy Policy

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by Google and Firebase as necessary to provide authentication, database storage, and related services. We do not intentionally share employment records with unrelated third parties for their own marketing.

6. Data Security

We use appropriate technical measures and available Firebase security features to help protect information. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

You may request account and associated data deletion by emailing us at supporttatisakilabs@gmail.com.

Please include "Overtime Tracker BD Account Deletion Request" in the subject line and provide the email address associated with your account. We may request reasonable verification before processing the request.

We will review and process valid requests and delete associated personal data, subject to applicable legal obligations. Deleting an authentication account alone may not automatically delete all associated database records; we will address applicable associated data as part of the deletion process.

8. Children's Privacy

The application is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.
